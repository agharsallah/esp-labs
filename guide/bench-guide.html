Between lessons

  1. Ctrl+C closes the monitor on the Mac only. The board keeps running.
  2. Unplug USB: this is what stops the program and the screen
  3. Rewire while off
  4. Check VCC is on 3V3, no loose ends
  5. Plug in, flash the next lesson
